Introduction

Hi! I'm Dzulfiqar Hanif, a Web Developer dedicated to building interfaces with soul

From there, I started self-taught learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Every project is a new challenge that always excites me — from tidy layouts to animations that make people say "wow".

I bridge the gap between structural precision and aesthetic emotion. To me, every line of code is a persona—a manifestation of an idea into a living digital experience. I thrive on turning complex visions into clean, 'wow-factor' realities without the clutter

Driven by the belief that gameplay is art, I strive to create meaningful digital spaces that are as functional as they are beautiful.
